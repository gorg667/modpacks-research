Glossary of modded Minecraft terms

Every piece of jargon used on this site — loaders, pack types, the big mods, and community shorthand — explained in a sentence or two.

Pack types

Kitchen sink — A pack that includes a large number of mods with minimal recipe changes or gating. Sandbox-first; you choose what to do. ATM, Enigmatica mainline, Direwolf20.

Expert pack — A pack whose recipes are heavily modified so mods depend on each other and progression is gated. Hard by design, usually with a quest book. E2E, DJ2, GTNH, Nomifactory.

Progression / questing pack — Designed around a quest book and staged unlocks but not necessarily hard. StoneBlock 4, Create: Astral, SevTech.

Skyblock — You start on a small island in the void and generate resources from nothing (sieves, resource trees, chickens). SkyFactory, ATM: To the Sky, PO3's Garden of Glass.

StoneBlock / OceanBlock / "-block" — Skyblock variants with a different environment: encased in stone, on a water planet, etc.

Vanilla+ — Enhances the base game without new systems to learn. Better Minecraft, Homestead.

Kitchen sink with a story — Community shorthand for RPG packs that are really large mod lists with quests on top. Often aimed at Prominence II.

Pack modes — One pack, multiple difficulty configurations. PO3 (Normal/Titan/Kappa/Mythic), Monifactory and Nomi-CEu (Normal/Hard/Expert).

Ages / stages — Progression where entire tiers of items are invisible or unusable until unlocked. SevTech invented the popular form; Decursio and Plastek continue it.

Performance pack — Adds no content; bundles optimisation and graphics mods. Fabulously Optimized, Simply Optimized, Additive, Adrenaline.

Loaders and versions

Forge — The original mod loader (2011–). Dominant through 1.20.1.

NeoForge — A 2023 fork of Forge by most of its former team; the standard for 1.20.4+ and effectively all new 1.21 packs.

Fabric — A lightweight loader (2018–) favoured for performance and quick updates. Sodium, Iris and many Vanilla+ packs live here.

Quilt — A Fabric fork; largely dormant for packs.

1.7.10 / 1.12.2 / 1.16.5 / 1.18.2 / 1.20.1 / 1.21.1 — The "long-term" modding versions where the mod ecosystem matured. Each hosts a generation of packs; packs rarely move between them.

Cleanroom — A modernised 1.12.2 loader enabling Java 21+ (MeatballCraft, Nomi-CEu). lwjgl3ify — GTNH's equivalent for 1.7.10.

Tools you'll see in every pack

JEI / EMI / NEI / REI — Recipe browsers ("Just/Roughly/Not Enough Items", EMI). Press R on an item for recipes, U for uses.

FTB Quests / Better Questing (BQ) — The quest-book mods. FTB Quests is the modern standard; BQ powered 1.12 packs like PO3 and GTNH.

KubeJS / CraftTweaker — Scripting mods packs use to change recipes and add custom mechanics. When a review says "custom", this is usually how.

JourneyMap / Xaero's — Minimaps and world maps.

Jade / WAILA / HWYLA — "What am I looking at" tooltips.

Polymorph — Lets you choose between conflicting recipes with the same ingredients. Its presence is a tell that recipes conflict.

The big mods

Create — Rotational mechanical automation: cogs, belts, presses, trains. The defining mod of 1.18–1.21 tech packs. Create 6 (2025) added a logistics network.

Mekanism — Industrial processing, ore multiplication, fusion reactors, the MekaSuit. Notoriously powerful when untweaked ("untweaked Mekanism" = a kitchen sink's difficulty).

Applied Energistics 2 (AE2) — Digital item storage and autocrafting via a network with channels. Refined Storage — the simpler alternative.

Thermal series / EnderIO / Immersive Engineering (IE) — Classic RF-power tech mods; IE is the multiblock, aesthetic one.

GregTech — The ultra-detailed industrial mod: realistic chemistry, voltage tiers (LV → MAX), multiblocks, micro-crafting. GT5U (1.7.10, GTNH), GTCE/GTCEu (1.12.2, Nomi), GTCEu Modern (1.20.1, Moni/TFG). "Greg-ified" = rebalanced around GregTech.

Modern Industrialization (MI) — Fabric's cleaner GregTech-like. StaTech.

Botania — Flower-based magic-tech with strong automation puzzles. Thaumcraft — 1.7/1.12 research-driven magic; the genre's benchmark. Blood Magic, Astral Sorcery, Ars Nouveau (modern spellcrafting), Occultism (rituals and summoning), Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Malum, Eidolon, Hexerei, Forbidden & Arcanus — other magic mods.

Draconic Evolution — Late-game energy storage and OP gear. Avaritia — "Infinity" tier items requiring absurd quantities; the classic expert-pack endgame.

Mystical Agriculture — Grow resources as crops. Chickens / Fluid Cows — breed animals that drop resources. Productive Bees — bees that produce resources. These are the resource-gen backbone of skyblocks and ATM.

Ex Nihilo (Sequentia/Ordo) — Sieves, barrels and crucibles: the traditional skyblock resource loop. Unearthers (SB4) and resource trees (SF4) are alternatives.

Twilight Forest / The Aether / Undergarden / Blue Skies / Ad Astra (space) — Dimension mods.

Epic Fight / Better Combat — Combat overhauls (animated third-person lock-on vs. lighter attack animations). Mine and Slash — Full ARPG stats/loot system (CtE2). Apotheosis — Affix gear, enchanting rework, bosses; a pack's "real" gear progression in many sinks.

Cobblemon / Pixelmon — Pokémon mods (voxel-style vs high-poly).

TerraFirmaCraft (TFC) — Realistic survival overhaul (geology, knapping, seasons).

Lost Cities — Procedural ruined-city worldgen (DeceasedCraft).

Sodium / Embeddium / Rubidium / Iris / Oculus / OptiFine — Renderers and shader loaders; see performance.

Community shorthand

ATM Star — ATM's creative-tier endgame item; "getting the Star" = finishing an ATM pack.

Stargate — GTNH's endgame construction; thousands of hours.

Kappa / Mythic — PO3's expert modes.

E2E / E6E / E9E / E10 — Enigmatica 2: Expert, 6: Expert, 9: Expert, 10.

A&B / CA&B / CABIN — Create: Above and Beyond (and its unofficial 1.18 port).

Nomi / Moni / Omni — Nomifactory / Monifactory / Omnifactory.

SB4 / OB2 / SF4 / PO3 / DJ2 / RotN / VH3 / CtE2 / MBC / TFG — StoneBlock 4, OceanBlock 2, SkyFactory 4, Project Ozone 3, Divine Journey 2, Rebirth of the Night, Vault Hunters 3, Craft to Exile 2, MeatballCraft, TerraFirmaGreg.

LPS — LunaPixelStudios, publisher of Better Minecraft, Medieval MC, Cottage Witch and (formerly) Prominence; a byword for large, lightly configured packs.

FTB — Feed The Beast, the oldest pack team (StoneBlock, Skies, OceanBlock, Direwolf20, Infinity Evolved). Also the name of the subreddit r/feedthebeast, which covers all modded Minecraft.

TPS — Ticks per second; 20 is perfect. Server-side lag shows here. FPS — frames per second; client-side.

Chunk loader — A block that keeps an area loaded when no player is nearby. Essential for automation; deadly for TPS.

Pregen — Pregenerating chunks (Chunky) so exploration doesn't stutter.
