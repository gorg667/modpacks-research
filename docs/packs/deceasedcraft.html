What's great

  • A real city: The Lost Cities generation with 60+ handcrafted buildings by a professional architect, five distinct districts, 800+ loot tables
  • Escalating hordes tied to your individual playtime; 15+ special infected; infection you can cure or succumb to
  • Tech progression via Create and Immersive Engineering that lets skilled players automate almost everything; vehicles to restore or build
  • 8+ NPCs with quests that unlock trading, a full questline and backstory, custom skill tree, diet-based hearts, parkour, body-part damage, thirst, temperature and seasons
  • Easy/Normal/Hard scaling; 100+ weapons/guns, 20+ armour sets across 4 tiers

What holds it back

  • v6.0 beta is the default download for some — it's missing the Wasteland and Abyss districts and can't be completed; grab v5.5.5 for a full run
  • Heavy: 8 GB recommended, 10 GB for the Distant Horizons build; city generation stutters on weak CPUs
  • No Nether or End by design; if you want Minecraft's dimensions, this isn't it
  • LAN play is discouraged — use the server pack

Play this if…

  • Project Zomboid / 7 Days to Die fans
  • Groups who want a tense base-defence-and-scavenge loop with a defined progression
  • Anyone who wants horror with systems rather than jump scares

Skip it if…

  • You want a light or relaxed pack
  • Your PC is below 16 GB total RAM

Overview

DeceasedCraft is the zombie pack that treats the apocalypse as a place. Its centrepiece is a procedurally generated city — The Lost Cities generation seeded with over sixty buildings hand-built by a professional architect — divided into five districts of rising danger, each with its own special infected, loot tables and NPCs. You claim a building, fortify it, scavenge, and progress through Create and Immersive Engineering tech toward vehicles, guns and ultimately the cure, while hordes scaled to your playtime escalate around you. Infection is real: get bitten and you cure it with a golden apple or turn.

It sits at 7 M+ downloads and in the global top 10 for most of 2025–26, and is the reference point for the whole "modern zombie apocalypse" category alongside The Cursed Walking. Compared to CW, DeceasedCraft is the more built experience — a designed city, NPC quests, a storyline, four armour tiers — and slightly more forgiving.

Versions

The current situation matters: v5.5.5 is the complete game. v5.10+ is an open beta of the v6.0 overhaul (new districts, new weapon playstyle, more guns and zombies) that explicitly cannot be finished — the Wasteland and Abyss districts aren't in yet. Pick accordingly. Servers need enable-command-block=true; don't add OptiFine (Embeddium is included).

Verdict

A-tier: the most complete and polished survival-horror pack on modern Minecraft. Heavy and not for weak PCs, but if scavenging a dead city with friends sounds like fun, nothing does it better.
