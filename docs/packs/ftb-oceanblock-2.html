What's great

  • Strong atmosphere: an ocean planet, underwater seed-planting, glass-dome dungeons, seabed bosses, a menu theme composed by the designer
  • Introduces unfamiliar mods well: Oritech, NauTec (custom), Just Dire Things, Integrated Dynamics
  • Early tools (sluices, jars) upgrade and stay relevant rather than being replaced
  • Runs smoothly; excellent for a first 1.21 pack

What holds it back

  • ‘Crafting simulator’: airdrops and quest rewards hand out Mystical Agriculture seeds and gear that skip most automation
  • Many big mods (IE, Extreme Reactors, Powah, Ars, Occultism) have 1–2 recipes of relevance
  • Endgame is ‘craft a list’ — the AE2 quantum core alone was 8 of one reviewer's 36 hours
  • NauTec's deep-water mechanics (crush depth, kelp, mobs) frustrate more than they challenge
  • No Create — it wasn't on 1.21 at release

Play this if…

  • A relaxed group month with a fresh theme
  • Players who want to touch Oritech and modern NeoForge tech without ATM's weight

Skip it if…

  • You want gating and automation pressure — StoneBlock 4 is the far better FTB pack

Overview

OceanBlock 2 opens with your escape pod crashing onto a water planet. From a floating platform you sluice, plant underwater, build Oritech and Mekanism machinery, dive into glass-dome dungeons and seabed boss arenas, and eventually reach the Rift — a dark dimension below 150 m that demands a Mk2 pressure suit (or, as players discovered, an invisibility-granting Mage robe). It's stylish, smooth and finishes in roughly 36 hours with side quests. The community verdict was fair and consistent: "pretty light in all ways — playtime, grind and performance," with too many mods that barely matter, too-generous airdrops, and an endgame that's a shopping list. FTB's designer engaged openly with the criticism, and StoneBlock 4 eight months later showed the lessons were learned.

Verdict

B-tier: a well-made, undemanding waterworld that's easy to like and easy to finish. If you want the same team at full strength, play StoneBlock 4.
