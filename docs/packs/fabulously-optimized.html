What's great

  • Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, ModernFix-class optimisation: 2–5× vanilla FPS on most hardware
  • OptiFine parity without OptiFine: shaders (Iris), connected textures, custom entity/item models, capes, zoom, dynamic lights, better grass
  • Zero configuration; sensible defaults; works on servers (client-side only)
  • Updated for new Minecraft versions faster than anything else in the ecosystem
  • Available on CurseForge, Modrinth, Prism Launcher's built-in list and as a vanilla-launcher installer

What holds it back

  • Adds no content — if you want ‘a modpack’ this isn't one
  • Fabric only; not usable as a base for Forge/NeoForge content packs
  • Some legacy OptiFine resource packs still need tweaks

Play this if…

  • Anyone playing vanilla survival, SMPs or minigame servers who wants better FPS and shaders
  • Low-end PCs and laptops
  • A safe base to add a few Fabric mods on top of

Skip it if…

  • You want new blocks, mobs, dimensions or progression
  • You need Forge/NeoForge mods — see Adrenaline for that side

The short version

Fabulously Optimized (FO) is the most popular "make Minecraft run well" pack — 18th most-downloaded on CurseForge, one of Modrinth's most downloaded projects of any type, built into Prism Launcher's pack list. It bundles the modern Fabric performance stack (Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling, Dynamic FPS, ModernFix-class fixes) with the graphics and QoL features people used to install OptiFine for (Iris shaders, Continuity connected textures, CIT/CEM via Chime/EMF/ETF, Capes, Zoomify, LambDynamicLights, Cull Leaves, Fabrishot) and sane defaults. Install it, play vanilla, enjoy 2–5× the frame rate and working shaders.

It is on this site because "which modpack should I play" is often secretly "how do I make Minecraft not run like garbage", and because it's the correct base for people who want vanilla plus a handful of mods.

Why FO over the alternatives

  • Simply Optimized — pure performance, no visual features, and adds C2ME/Fastload/MoreCulling for theoretically higher throughput. Marginally faster in benchmarks, but you lose connected textures, capes, zoom, dynamic lights. Pick it if you literally only want FPS.
  • Additive (Fabric) / Adrenaline (Forge/NeoForge) — the same developer's "vanilla-feel + performance" packs; Adrenaline is the answer for anyone who needs a Forge/NeoForge base.
  • Sodium alone — most of the FPS gain with none of the QoL; fine if you know what you're doing.
  • OptiFine — still works for its fans, but is slower than Sodium on modern hardware, closed source, and lags Minecraft releases by weeks or months. FO is its spiritual replacement and the community consensus recommendation.

Setup notes

Install through Prism Launcher (search the built-in list), the Modrinth App, or CurseForge. 2–4 GB allocated is plenty; more does nothing. Shaders: drop a pack (Complementary, BSL, Photon…) into the shaderpacks folder and enable under Video Settings → Shader Packs. It's client-only — join any vanilla server with it.

Verdict

FO isn't a game, so its content score is irrelevant; on the axes that matter for a utility pack — polish, speed, compatibility, update cadence — it's a 10 across the board. If you play vanilla or near-vanilla, this should be your launcher's default profile.
