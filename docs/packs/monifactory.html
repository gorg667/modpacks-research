What's great

  • Everything that made Nomifactory great — grind-free GT, encyclopedic quests, Peaceful-friendly — on a modern version with GTCEu Modern
  • Complete overhaul of UV+ progression; Draconic Evolution and Avaritia replaced with sculk-based mechanics
  • In-menu mode switcher: Normal / Hard (steam age, no HNN, unspendable coins, harder Pt/naquadah) / Expert (no RF→EU, GT5u-style explosions, LaserIO disabled)
  • Optional compat for Create, CC:Tweaked, XNet, Flux Networks, Compact Machines and more, with recipes auto-adjusted
  • Full retexture for a coherent modern look

What holds it back

  • Pre-1.0: balance and content still shifting; some rough edges Nomi-CEu doesn't have
  • Heavier than Nomi (6–8 GB vs 3–4)
  • Community slightly split between Nomi-CEu and Moni, thinning guides for each
  • Same ‘Only Factories’ narrowness as Nomi

Play this if…

  • Players who want Nomifactory but refuse 1.12.2
  • Nomi-CEu veterans wanting a new endgame and Expert mode
  • GregTech-curious players stepping up from StaTech or E9E

Skip it if…

  • You want the proven, finished version — Nomi-CEu
  • You want GTNH's scale — this is a fifth of it

Overview

Monifactory is explicitly "a remaster and port of Nomifactory CEu using GregTech CEu Modern" — the same grind-free, quest-guided factory campaign on Forge 1.20.1, with several genuine changes: a rebuilt UV+ endgame (sculk mechanics replace Draconic Evolution and Avaritia), new multiblocks, a whole-pack retexture, and a proper Expert difficulty for returning Nomi Hard Mode players. Since 0.13 you switch Normal/Hard/Expert from the main menu; servers switch via the MoniLabs jar.

Community difficulty threads place it between Enigmatica 2: Expert and Nomi-CEu — "a bit more than Nomi" — and it's frequently the first name offered when someone asks for a modern expert pack ("well, there's Monifactory").

Notes

  • Hard mode brings back the steam age, removes Hostile Neural Networks (no free infinite mob drops), makes Monicoins unspendable, hardens platinum and naquadah lines and emphasises GT power generation. Expert disables RF→EU conversion (GT power only), disables LaserIO and re-enables GT5u-style machine explosions.
  • Optional add-on compat (drop-in mods with auto-adjusted recipes): Create (+ Steam 'n' Rails, Deco), ComputerCraft: Tweaked (+ Advanced Peripherals), Tempad, XNet, Flux Networks, Modular Routers, Compact Machines, Architect's Palette.
  • 6–8 GB client; servers 8 GB. Forge 47.4.x.

Verdict

The right GregTech pack for 2026 if version matters to you, and a real design step forward at the top end. A-tier rather than S only because it's still pre-1.0 and Nomi-CEu remains the more polished, better-documented experience today.
