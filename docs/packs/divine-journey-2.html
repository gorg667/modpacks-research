What's great

  • Truly mixed expert design: Thaumcraft, Botania, Blood Magic, Astral Sorcery, Bewitchment and Totemic are as load-bearing as Mekanism, IE, Thermal, EnderIO and AE2
  • 25 chapters with a genuine narrative through-line and a finale that requires everything you've built
  • Forces you to automate every resource — the defining ‘you cannot hand-craft your way through this’ experience
  • Extremely polished and bug-free for its size; excellent quest descriptions
  • Beloved by finishers: ‘the best pack I've ever played’ is the modal review

What holds it back

  • Grind is real: singularities at 100 M RF each, awakened Draconium at 1.8 B RF, dozens of ‘make 1,000 of this’ steps
  • 1.12.2 and Java 8
  • Very little exploration or combat payoff; mid-game AE2 unlock is a long way in
  • Some ‘make your own fun’ stretches where the quest gives a target and nothing else
  • Solo completion is a six-month project for most

Play this if…

  • Players who finished E2E and want the next mountain without GregTech
  • Magic-mod fans who want their Thaumcraft/Botania/Blood Magic to *matter* in an expert pack
  • Anyone who enjoys designing enormous automation webs across a dozen mods

Skip it if…

  • You want a 100-hour pack
  • You dislike gates that demand enormous quantities rather than clever design
  • You need modern Minecraft

Overview

Divine Journey 2 is the 1.12.2 heavyweight of the expert genre. Its pitch — "find the meaning of life" — plays out over 25 chapters in which each mod's gate demands products of the others: Thaumcraft infusion needs tech-tier metals; Draconic Evolution needs Botania and Blood Magic dragon hearts; AE2 arrives only mid-game after you've proven you can automate without it; and the final crafts pull from everything. Community difficulty rankings put it just under MeatballCraft and GTNH, and above E6E and PO3 Kappa.

Finishers report ~540 hours for 100%, and a common ~500-hour figure for a complete run by a returning modded player. Comments like "took me six months" and "after 400 hours my friend and I are halfway through the quest book" are typical.

What it demands

The signature is scale. A player wanting an emerald danknull found the chain: singularities (100 M RF and 1,000 items each) → Singularification Catalyst → Awakened Core (100 M RF) → Awakened Draconium (1.8 B RF, fusion crafting, D-T fuel) → dragon hearts (mid/late Botania and Blood Magic) → Thaumcraft infusion. Everything is like this. Whether that's "epic scale" or "grind" is the whole debate around the pack, and both camps are right.

What isn't debated: it's polished. Bugs are rare, quest text is clear, recipes are consistent, and the finale lands.

Compared

  • vs SevTech: Ages: SevTech is a staged caveman-to-space story with a rough endgame; DJ2 is a pure automation gauntlet with a satisfying one. Most players find SevTech more fun and DJ2 more rewarding.
  • vs Project Ozone 3 (Kappa/Mythic): PO3 is skyblock with adjustable difficulty and a sillier tone; DJ2 is a world pack with a fixed, serious curve.
  • vs Enigmatica 2: Expert: E2E is the gentler sibling — same magic+tech philosophy, roughly half the length. Play E2E first.
  • vs GTNH: DJ2 is about one-fifth of GTNH's runtime and has no GregTech.

Verdict

A-tier: one of the greatest expert packs ever made, with the reservation that its difficulty is often quantity rather than cleverness. If E2E left you wanting more, this is more.
