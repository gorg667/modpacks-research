What's great

  • Unique arc: TFC's stone-age realism (knapping, pit kilns, seasons, nutrition, real geology) flows into GregTech's voltage tiers without a seam
  • Ore is where geology says it is; food rots; winter kills — survival that means something for hundreds of hours
  • GTCEu Modern's full multiblock ladder from steam to LuV+ on 1.20.1
  • Active dev team, growing roadmap (space), engaged r/TerraFirmaGreg community
  • Stunning TFC worldgen and immersion

What holds it back

  • ‘It's 90% TerraFirmaCraft+ for a long while until you advance into GregTech’ — if you want Greg fast, this isn't it
  • No real endgame yet: goals are an Ultimate Battery and a ‘Not A Noob Certificate’; a Stargate-equivalent is explicitly not planned
  • Two of the least forgiving mods in Minecraft stacked; new-to-both players report ~150 h to reach MV
  • Heavy and occasionally rough around the edges as it's under active development

Play this if…

  • TFC fans who want a purpose beyond survival
  • GregTech veterans who want a completely different early game
  • Players who love slow, meaningful, realistic progression and long sessions

Skip it if…

  • You want to reach machines in your first weekend
  • You need a defined finish line
  • You want a friendly early game — this is the hardest opening on the site

Overview

TerraFirmaCraft (TFC) is the realism mod: geology-based ore, knapping stone tools, pit-kiln ceramics, temperature-controlled metallurgy, nutrition and seasons. GregTech CEu Modern is the industrial mod. TerraFirmaGreg-Modern stitches them into one progression on Forge 1.20.1 — you start with a rock and a stick and end, hundreds of hours later, running an EBF fed by a TFC-mined, TFC-smelted supply chain and beyond into IV, LuV and up.

It was one of the most upvoted answers in the 2026 "top modpacks" thread ("TerraFirmaGreg!!") — with the top reply immediately attaching a disclaimer that has become the pack's unofficial motto: it needs a big warning that it's 90% just TerraFirmaCraft+ for a long while until you advance into GregTech. One commenter wished for a "TerraFirmaGregless" fork of the early game, which tells you how good that early game is and how long it lasts.

Progression & time

From r/TerraFirmaGreg reports: a player new to both mods hit early MV around 150 hours; an experienced TFC player new to Greg reached late IV by in-game year 10; a focused player was at LuV around 300 hours. The devs consider AE2 "about the halfway point". There is no completion state: the only defined goals are crafting the Ultimate Battery (don't try to fill it) and the tongue-in-cheek Not A Noob Certificate. Space content is planned for a future version; a Stargate is explicitly not — the working idea for a final goal is mining a neutron star for creative items.

The r/ftb expert-difficulty ranking places TFG above Nomi-CEu and Monifactory and just below MeatballCraft and GTNH.

Notes

  • 8 GB client; expect stutter during TFC chunk generation on older CPUs.
  • Multiplayer helps enormously (TFC's labour division; Greg's ore hunting).
  • The legacy TerraFirmaGreg (1.12.2) still exists; Modern is the one under development.

Verdict

A-tier: the most immersive ramp into industry in all of modded Minecraft, held back only by an unfinished endgame and the sheer length of the TFC phase. If you already love TFC, this is the reason to play it again; if you came for GregTech, play Nomi or Moni first and come back when you want the long way round.
