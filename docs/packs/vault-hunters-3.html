What's great

  • The Vault: procedurally generated, timed dungeon runs with 18 themes, objectives, modifiers and loot — Minecraft's best roguelike
  • RPG spine: levels, talents, abilities, expertise, Vault gear with affixes, jewels, and 25 artifacts to unlock the final challenge
  • Research system gates mods (Create, Botania, Mekanism, AE2, Refined Storage…) behind knowledge points — you *choose* your tech
  • Custom mod (the_vault) is genuinely professional: UI, models, balance passes, seasonal updates
  • Best multiplayer culture in modded Minecraft; Sky Vaults skyblock variant for a fresh start

What holds it back

  • Grind is the game: hundreds of runs for gear and levels; if the Vault loop doesn't click, nothing else will
  • Base building and mods are secondary — many players never leave the Vault
  • 1.18.2 with a heavy custom mod; performance dips in big vaults
  • Balance swings with each Update; dying loses your loot on the default setting

Play this if…

  • Groups who want a shared, endless co-op goal with loot to compare
  • ARPG/roguelike fans (Diablo, Risk of Rain, Hades)
  • Streamers and viewers — the pack is built for spectacle

Skip it if…

  • You want to build and automate more than you want to run dungeons
  • Repetition burns you out

Overview

Vault Hunters, from Hermitcraft's Iskall85 and a full development team, is the modpack-as-game: a custom mod builds the Vault, a procedurally generated dimension you enter through a crafted altar for timed, objective-based runs — kill the boss, complete monoliths, scavenge items, elixir rush — across 18 themes with random modifiers. You return (if you survive) with Vault gear, jewels, knowledge and experience. Levels unlock talents, abilities and expertise; knowledge points are spent to research the pack's normal mods (Create, Botania, Mekanism, Refined Storage, and dozens more), so your base tech is a build choice. Collect 25 artifacts to attempt the final Vault.

It's the only pack on this site where "what's the mod list?" is almost beside the point; the loop is the product, and after five years and 21 major updates it's exceptionally polished.

Wold's Vaults

An unofficial expansion built on VH3 (Update 16+), Wold's Vaults adds optimisation mods, QoL add-ons, new objective types, weapon types, uniques, catalysts and an augment table, ParCool movement and Better Combat, an extra quest book, and — importantly — an actual endgame roadmap. Community consensus in 2025–26 is that Wold's is "more enjoyable", "tons of improvements over base"; critics note a messier UI. VH3 worlds convert directly. If you're starting fresh, try Wold's.

Verdict

A-tier: the best co-op loop in modded Minecraft and a superbly built custom experience, whose appeal is entirely conditional on loving dungeon runs. Test it for ten hours; you'll know.
