What's great

  • Interlocked progression: Thaumcraft, Botania, Blood Magic and Astral Sorcery are as required as Mekanism, Thermal, IE and AE2, and each mod's gate uses the others' outputs sensibly
  • The quest book explains *why*, not just *what* — the best-written of the 1.12 expert packs
  • Difficulty through cleverness more than quantity; automating each step feels like a puzzle solved
  • Complete, polished, and still the community's #1 answer to ‘which expert pack first?’
  • Perfect stepping stone to Nomifactory, DJ2 or GTNH

What holds it back

  • 1.12.2 / Java 8
  • Late game leans on a few heavy grinds (Avaritia-adjacent, Draconic Evolution) that feel less elegant than the mid-game
  • Performance is 1.12-typical: 6–8 GB, long loads

Play this if…

  • Your first expert pack — full stop
  • Players who want magic and tech to be equal partners
  • Anyone who found ATM-style packs pointless and wants recipes that push back

Skip it if…

  • You refuse 1.12.2 — Enigmatica 9: Expert is the modern take
  • You want GregTech specifically

Overview

Enigmatica 2: Expert (E2E) is the pack that, in nearly every Reddit thread about expert packs since 2019, someone calls "one of the best packs ever made." It is a 1.12.2 mixed-mod expert pack: roughly 270 mods with heavily modified recipes so that no mod can be progressed in isolation — Thaumcraft needs Immersive Engineering steel, Mekanism needs Botania mana-infused materials, AE2 needs Blood Magic, and so on — culminating in creative items and the Infinity-tier finale.

What sets it apart from its peers is tone: E2E is hard because it makes you think, not (mostly) because it makes you wait. Its quest book explains reasoning and points at solutions, its gates are usually one clever automation away, and progression rarely stalls for lack of information. The community difficulty ladder consistently places it as the first "real" expert pack — above Create: A&B, below Nomifactory and E6E — and as the entry point to the whole genre ("e2e > nomi > gtnh").

Compared

  • Enigmatica 6: Expert (1.16.5) — the bigger, harder sequel; excellent, notably tougher, and where E2E fans go next if they want to stay in the series.
  • Enigmatica 9: Expert (1.19.2) — the modern take with the same philosophy on newer mods; some feel it's less tight than E2E.
  • Divine Journey 2 — the next mountain: twice the length, more quantity-grind.
  • Nomifactory — the GregTech on-ramp; play after E2E if GTNH is the goal.

Verdict

S-tier for its role: the best-designed on-ramp into expert Minecraft ever made, and still the correct first expert pack in 2026 despite its age. If the version bothers you, E9E carries the torch; if it doesn't, start here.
