What's great

  • Two complete games in one install: ATM's full tech/magic roster and Cobblemon with add-ons
  • ATM polish and update cadence; huge community support
  • Great for servers where half the group wants factories and half wants a Pokédex

What holds it back

  • Little integration between Pokémon and tech — parallel sandboxes, not a designed hybrid
  • Heavy (8–10 GB); ATM-typical lack of gating
  • No gyms/League structure — Cobblemon is a feature here, not a campaign

Play this if…

  • Mixed groups torn between ATM10 and a Cobblemon pack

Skip it if…

  • You want a Pokémon *game* — COBBLEVERSE

Overview

ATMons is exactly what the name suggests: the ATM team's NeoForge 1.21.1 kitchen sink with Cobblemon and its add-on ecosystem dropped in. With 1.4 M downloads it leads the Cobblemon spin-off field by a wide margin and holds a top-10 global slot. What it does well is breadth — every ATM mod plus Pokémon, all polished and stable. What it lacks is design: the Pokémon layer and the tech layer run in parallel, there's no gym progression, and the ATM quest book treats Cobblemon as one more chapter. For servers of mixed tastes it's a genuinely useful compromise; for anyone who wants a Pokémon RPG it's the wrong tool.

Verdict

B-tier: two good sandboxes bolted together. Pick COBBLEVERSE for Pokémon, ATM10 for tech, ATMons only if you truly want both at once.
