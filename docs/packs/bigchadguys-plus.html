Overview

BigChadGuys Plus is the cozy Cobblemon pack: a "casual everyday life" loop of farming, decorating, selling crops and exploring, with Pokémon as companions rather than the sole objective. Built-in shaders and quests give it a Stardew-like warmth; a small studio maintains it with a wiki and Discord. Hosting guides consistently file it as "the comfy one" among Cobblemon packs, and that's right. Its main limitation is the 1.20.1 Forge base, which trails Cobblemon's newest content on 1.21.

Verdict

A-tier within its niche: the best relaxed Cobblemon pack. Pair COBBLEVERSE (campaign) and BCG+ (cozy) as the two ends of the Pokémon spectrum.
