What's great

  • Resource trees: grow saplings into iron, redstone, even Nether-tier materials — an ingenious, relaxing core loop
  • Prestige mode: optional meta-progression across runs that unlocks mods and features
  • Dozens of world types (classic tree, Lost Cities, Cave, Skyblock variants) and a huge advancement book
  • Light, stable, welcoming; the #4 most-downloaded pack ever for good reason

What holds it back

  • 1.12.2 / Java 8
  • Little challenge — an easy, cosy sink rather than a designed gauntlet
  • SkyFactory 5 has not replaced it, and probably won't

Play this if…

  • First skyblock; relaxed group servers; players who like tidy, self-contained loops

Skip it if…

  • You want difficulty or modern Minecraft

Overview

SkyFactory 4 replaced Ex Nihilo sieving with resource trees — saplings that grow logs of iron, gold, redstone and dozens more — and wrapped it in a prestige meta-game, a hundred world-type options and a gigantic advancement book. It is the fourth most-downloaded modpack ever and remains the community's default "relaxed skyblock" recommendation seven years later. It's light (4–6 GB), stable, and has no real pain points beyond its version and a lack of challenge — which is the point.

SkyFactory 5 (Forge 1.20.1, Mar 2025) exists: a colour/dye-themed resource system, Create's SkyFactory debut, gateway boss trophies for on-demand mob spawning, and a task book. Reception has been quiet and updates stalled after March 2025; it's worth a look for the new ideas, but SF4 remains the recommendation.

Verdict

A-tier: the best relaxed skyblock, still. Play SF4 for the classic; peek at SF5 out of curiosity.
