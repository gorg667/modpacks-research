What's great

  • Modern Industrialization: a clean, well-documented GregTech-style tiered machine mod without the micro-crafting misery
  • 750+ quests with a heavily modified progression that actually teaches
  • Fabric + Sodium: very light and smooth for a tech pack
  • Named the easiest of the expert-adjacent factory packs in community rankings — by design

What holds it back

  • Shorter and shallower than Nomi/Moni; veterans finish quickly
  • MI's ecosystem is smaller than GregTech's

Play this if…

  • Players curious about factory packs but scared of GregTech
  • Fabric users who want a proper tech progression

Skip it if…

  • You want the full Greg experience — Nomifactory or Monifactory

Overview

StaTech Industry is the Fabric world's answer to Nomifactory: a quest-driven factory pack built on Modern Industrialization, a mod that takes GregTech's tiered-machine, multiblock, fluid-and-circuit gameplay and rebuilds it with modern UI, clear documentation and far less tedium. With 750+ quests and a heavily reworked progression, it teaches automation step by step; community difficulty threads rank it the easiest of the GregTech-adjacent packs ("statech is easiest since it's MI and not GT — but that being easiest shows how hard this list is"). StaTech Industry 2 carries the idea to newer versions with MI's updated toolset.

Verdict

A-tier: the friendliest serious factory pack, and the right first rung on the ladder that leads to Nomi, Moni and eventually GTNH.
