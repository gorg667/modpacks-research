## "What's the best modpack?"

There isn't one. There's a best pack *for what you want*. If you're forcing us: [ATM10](../packs/all-the-mods-10.html) for a group that wants everything, [StoneBlock 4](../packs/ftb-stoneblock-4.html) for a group that wants a goal, [GTNH](../packs/gregtech-new-horizons.html) for the most ambitious thing ever built in Minecraft. Read [How to choose](how-to-choose.html) or take the [quiz](../quiz.html).

## "What's a good first modpack?"

[Better Minecraft](../packs/better-minecraft-bmc4.html) if you want more Minecraft; [Homestead](../packs/homestead.html) if you want cozy; [SkyFactory 4](../packs/skyfactory-4.html) if you like a self-contained loop; [Create: Astral](../packs/create-astral.html) if you want to learn automation gently; [COBBLEVERSE](../packs/cobbleverse.html) if you want Pokémon.

## "What's the hardest modpack?"

[GregTech: New Horizons](../packs/gregtech-new-horizons.html), and it isn't close — 1,600–2,500 hours to the endgame, 4,000+ to the Stargate. After it: [MeatballCraft](../packs/meatballcraft.html), [TerraFirmaGreg](../packs/terrafirmagreg.html), [Divine Journey 2](../packs/divine-journey-2.html), Nomifactory CEu (Hard), PO3 Mythic, E6E. For *survival* difficulty rather than recipe difficulty: [RLCraft](../packs/rlcraft.html) and [Rebirth of the Night](../packs/rebirth-of-the-night.html).

## "I want to work up to GTNH. What order?"

The community ladder: **E2E → Nomifactory (or Monifactory) → GTNH.** Optionally StaTech before E2E if you've never done a factory pack, and DJ2 or MeatballCraft between Nomi and GTNH if you want a non-Greg detour. The GTNH wiki itself recommends Nomifactory as preparation.

## "Why are so many great packs on 1.12.2 / 1.7.10?"

Because expert packs take years to build and Minecraft versions turn over faster than that. 1.12.2 was a stable target from 2017 to ~2020, and mods stopped porting away from it, so pack makers could finish. GTNH has had ten years on 1.7.10. Modern versions churn every 12–18 months, mods chase the newest one, and large curated packs never get to mature. 1.20.1 may become the next long-lived home; NeoForge 1.21.1 is the current one. Refusing to play older versions cuts you off from most of the best-designed packs ever made.

## "Forge, NeoForge or Fabric — which should I pick?"

You don't pick; the pack does, and the launcher installs it. NeoForge is where new 1.21 packs are; Forge 1.20.1 is the biggest ecosystem; Fabric is lighter and hosts the performance and many Vanilla+ packs. Don't choose a pack by loader.

## "How much RAM do I need?"

See the table in [How to choose](how-to-choose.html#step-3-check-your-hardware). Short version: 8 GB system RAM limits you to light packs (4–5 GB allocated); 16 GB runs almost everything (6–8 GB allocated) except ATM/MeatballCraft comfortably; 32 GB runs anything. Never allocate more than half your physical RAM.

## "Why does my game freeze every few seconds?"

Usually garbage collection from a badly sized heap — too much or too little RAM. Fix the allocation first; see [performance](performance-tuning.html). MeatballCraft specifically needs its published JVM flags.

## "CurseForge won't install GTNH / it's out of date."

Correct. The GTNH team says not to use CurseForge. Download from [gtnewhorizons.com](https://www.gtnewhorizons.com/) and use Prism Launcher, MultiMC, ATLauncher or Technic.

## "Is X pack dead?"

Check the "Status" line in its review. Frozen packs (A&B, Arcane Engineering, E2E, DJ2, SevTech, RLCraft) are complete and still excellent; "dead" only matters if the pack was unfinished. SkyFactory 5 and E10 Expert are the two "watch this space" cases in 2026.

## "Prominence II looks amazing. Why do people hate it?"

They don't all hate it — it's a top-10 pack with a loyal base. The most-upvoted criticism concerns mismatched tech mods in a fantasy setting, configuration issues (recipe conflicts, crafting lag from compat mods), swingy boss design and classes that matter less than advertised. The 4.0 Kingdom of Vaaz update (Aug 2026) addressed a lot. Our [review](../packs/prominence-2.html) lays out both sides; [Craft to Exile 2](../packs/craft-to-exile-2.html) and [Integrated MC](../packs/integrated-mc.html) are the usual alternatives.

## "Cobblemon or Pixelmon?"

Cobblemon for modern Minecraft, Minecraft-style models and the active ecosystem ([COBBLEVERSE](../packs/cobbleverse.html) for a campaign); [Pixelmon](../packs/pixelmon.html) for high-poly nostalgia and established servers.

## "Which Create pack?"

Learn: [Create: Astral](../packs/create-astral.html). Pure challenge: [Above and Beyond](../packs/create-above-and-beyond.html). Challenge with magic: [Arcane Engineering](../packs/create-arcane-engineering.html). Everything modern: [All of Create](../packs/all-of-create.html). Something strange and brilliant: [Liminal Industries](../packs/liminal-industries.html).

## "Is ATM10 too hard for beginners?"

It isn't hard at all — it's *big*. The difficulty is choice paralysis and JEI overload, not recipes. Pick one mod chain (Mekanism → AE2) and ignore the rest until you need them. If that still sounds overwhelming, start with StoneBlock 4 or Better Minecraft.

## "Can I add shaders?"

Almost always. Modern packs bundle Iris/Oculus with Sodium/Embeddium; drop a shader pack into `shaderpacks` and enable it in Video Settings. Never install OptiFine into a modpack. Budget 1–2 GB extra RAM and 30–60% of your FPS.

## "Solo or server?"

Anything works solo. Expert packs and GTNH are *much* better with a small team; kitchen sinks, Vault Hunters, COBBLEVERSE and the zombie packs shine on servers. Cozy and questing packs are great either way. Hosting: budget the client RAM figure + 2 GB, pregenerate chunks, and match pack versions exactly.

## "My friends and I can't agree."

ATM10 (everyone finds a mod), Vault Hunters (shared goal, individual builds), Project Ozone 3 (same pack, different difficulty modes), or Better Minecraft (nobody has to learn anything).
