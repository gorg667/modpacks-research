## Overview

MC Eternal (1.12.2) was the "run a town, explore dungeons, do magic, do tech" sink beloved by casual servers; **MC Eternal 2** (Forge 1.20.1, 2025) carries nearly all of what made it special — custom bosses, shops, dimensions, optional quests — with modern performance and long-term mod support. Community consensus is warm ("very fun and polished for more casual play", a fixture of 2026 recommendation threads) with the caveat that development isn't finished: entire quest chapters were still missing through 2025, and most mods remain unmodified.

## Verdict

B-tier: a good-natured, polished casual sink that will be better when its chapters land. Fine choice for a relaxed group; not for anyone chasing challenge.
