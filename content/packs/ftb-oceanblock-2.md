## Overview

OceanBlock 2 opens with your escape pod crashing onto a water planet. From a floating platform you sluice, plant underwater, build Oritech and Mekanism machinery, dive into glass-dome dungeons and seabed boss arenas, and eventually reach **the Rift** — a dark dimension below 150 m that demands a Mk2 pressure suit (or, as players discovered, an invisibility-granting Mage robe). It's stylish, smooth and finishes in roughly **36 hours** with side quests. The community verdict was fair and consistent: "pretty light in all ways — playtime, grind and performance," with too many mods that barely matter, too-generous airdrops, and an endgame that's a shopping list. FTB's designer engaged openly with the criticism, and StoneBlock 4 eight months later showed the lessons were learned.

## Verdict

B-tier: a well-made, undemanding waterworld that's easy to like and easy to finish. If you want the same team at full strength, play StoneBlock 4.
