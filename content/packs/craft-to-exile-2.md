## Overview

Craft to Exile 2 is the rare RPG pack where the systems come first. Its author also develops **Mine and Slash**, the ARPG mod at its core, and the pack is essentially that mod's showcase: pick a class, gain stats/skills/passives per level, traverse a Path-of-Exile-shaped talent tree, specialise into an ascendancy, hunt uniques (yes, the Stone of Jordan is in) and Diablo-II runewords (Chains of Honor), slot support gems into skills, and craft via professions that also feed the food and potion systems. Everything — cooking, brewing, scrolls — routes through Mine and Slash.

The **campaign** is real: 250+ quests across five acts plus prologue and epilogue, quest shops, MCA Reborn town-building, and dimensions gated by level (Overworld 1–15, Nether 10–30, Undergarden 25–40, Everbright/Everdawn 35–55, End 50–65, Otherside 60–75, Twilight Forest 90+). At level 50/Act V you unlock **Maps** — repeatable instanced dungeons with special mobs and loot — and the **2.0 Atlas Update** (August 2026) adds an Atlas web and passive tree to shape your endgame, Shrines, Strongboxes, imprisoned monsters, five new map types, 25 crafting materials, two new classes and a pinnacle "Apex" encounter. PCGamesN called it "Minecraft in a form almost completely unrecognisable from its traditional cozy vibes."

## Reception

Split along ARPG-fan lines. PoE players call it excellent and "the proper RPG pack" (it's the standard counter-recommendation in Prominence complaint threads). Players who want combat *feel* rather than combat *math* find that "adding skill points didn't change anything" and call it "buns". Both are describing the same design choice.

## Verdict

A-tier: the most coherent, complete and server-ready RPG modpack on modern Minecraft, provided you like your RPGs numeric. Pair it with Prominence II in your mind as the two poles of the genre — systems vs spectacle — and choose accordingly.
