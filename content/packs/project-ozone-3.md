## Overview

Project Ozone 3 completes a trilogy that popularised the **pack mode** idea: one modpack whose recipes and gating change with a config flag. **Normal** is a pleasant skyblock kitchen sink with a quest book. **Titan** turns up recipes. **Kappa** is a full expert pack where nearly everything routes through Avaritia's extreme crafting table and where the final Infinity items require you to automate output from dozens of mods. **Mythic** (2021) is "Kappa on steroids" — the same skeleton with the endgame made harder still (with some vanilla nerfs like 1 log → 1 plank, and, at launch, enough bugs that "it may not actually be harder").

World types are equally flexible: Garden of Glass skyblock, floating islands, the Lost Cities, or a normal overworld. The community difficulty ladder puts Normal near MC Eternal, Kappa around E6E, Mythic just under MeatballCraft.

## Notes

PO3's strength is choice and breadth, not curation — the same mod list serves every mode, so Normal has lots of pointless mods and Kappa has lots of grind. Its weakness is that Kappa/Mythic difficulty is mostly *quantity*. Still: for a group that wants to play "the same pack" at different intensities, nothing else does this as well, and the Avaritia endgame remains one of modded Minecraft's great flexes. Fans are still asking for PO4.

## Verdict

A-tier for flexibility and community; play Normal for a good sink, Kappa if you want an expert grind with a skyblock start.
