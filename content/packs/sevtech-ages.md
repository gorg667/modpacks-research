## Overview

SevTech: Ages (2018) is one of the most important modpacks ever released: it popularised **hard staged progression** with hidden items, built a coherent narrative — you are a caveman, and by the end you are on Mars — and proved a pack could be a *designed game*. It remains a top-15 all-time CurseForge download and the reference point for every "ages" pack since.

**Age 0** (Stone): Primal Core, flint knapping, hide, a fire pit; no ores visible. **Age 1** (Bronze/Tribal): Totemic rituals, Better With Mods hand-crank machinery, Astral Sorcery begins. **Age 2** (Medieval): iron, Blood Magic, Abyssalcraft, the Twilight Forest, the Beneath. **Age 3** (Industrial): Immersive Engineering, steel, Thermal. **Age 4** (Modern): Mekanism-class tech, Applied Energistics, EnderIO. **Age 5** (Futuristic): Galacticraft to the Moon and Mars, Draconic-style endgame. Each Age reveals ores and recipes that were previously *invisible* in the world and in JEI, which makes advancing feel like waking up.

## Praise and complaints

Praise centres on Ages 0–3: the caveman start is unforgettable, the tribal-to-medieval leap is superbly paced, and the pack's theming (Abyssalcraft as a horror undercurrent, Astral Sorcery as navigation) is unusually coherent. Complaints centre on **Age 5** — widely called grindy and underbaked — and on performance, which is heavy for 1.12.2. Community rankings place it around E2E/A&B difficulty "minus the end", and in 2021 comparisons players preferred it to DJ2 for fun while conceding DJ2 for depth.

## Legacy

SevTech's DNA is everywhere: **The Decursio Project** (1.16.5) is the most direct modern successor (ages, similar staging); **Plastek** (1.19.2, seven ages); **Chroma Technology/Endless**; and FTB's own **Interactions**. None has fully replaced it.

## Verdict

A-tier and essential history: the best first half of any progression pack, a weaker last act, and dated performance. If you want the same idea on modern Minecraft, look at Decursio; if you want the original, it still holds up — just be ready to look things up.
