## Overview

Enigmatica 10 is the non-expert Enigmatica for NeoForge 1.21.1: a broad tech-and-magic sandbox drawing from the same 2025–26 mod pool as ATM10, but with the Enigmatica team's characteristic curation — unified ores and materials, consistent tool tiers, recipe conflicts resolved, performance mods tuned, and a clean visual language. It moved out of long beta in 2026 (with a full release in August and frequent updates since) and is the pack to choose when "ATM but tidier and lighter" is the brief.

Its weaknesses are the flip side: fewer mods than ATM10, and — as of late summer 2026 — a quest book and endgame still growing. Historically Enigmatica's mainline packs are the foundation for their Expert versions, so E10 Expert is the thing to watch.

## Verdict

A-tier kitchen sink: the most polished modern sandbox short of ATM10's sheer size, and the better pick for mid-range hardware or anyone allergic to duplicate ores.
