## The short version

Create: Above and Beyond (CA&B or A&B) is the official Create modpack, released by the Create team in late 2021 on Forge 1.16.5. It has one idea and executes it perfectly: **each chapter names a product, and you must build a factory that makes it in quantity.** Andesite alloy → brass → precision mechanisms → sturdy sheets → sealed mechanisms → calculation mechanisms… roughly fifty "inventions" later you assemble a **space shuttle** and launch. There is no dragon, no bosses, no magic — just a rising curve of logistics problems, each one a little too big to hand-craft, each one solvable only by designing a production line on paper before you place a block.

Five years on, it is still the reference point: every Create-progression pack since (Arcane Engineering, Astral, All of Create, Prepare to Dye) is described in relation to it, and the 2026 consensus guide to Create packs still calls it "the benchmark of the genre" and "for players who want demanding pure automation".

## How it plays

The pack is small (~110 mods): Create (0.3.2-era), Immersive Engineering-lite elements, Thermal Foundation materials, Tinkers'-free, a few exploration and building mods, and a lot of **custom content** — Above-and-Beyond-exclusive fluids (like liquid "slag" chains), blocks, items and KubeJS scripts written by the Create developers for this pack.

**Chapters.** Each chapter's "core" item (e.g. Andesite Machine, Brass Machine) is the gate; once you can make it, it crafts directly into that tier's basic machines — a deliberate design that keeps recipes for the *basics* cheap while making the *core* the engineering challenge. Quests hand you the target and a hint; the wiki and community fill in the rest.

**Scale.** Early chapters can be done with a handful of machines. By the middle, you are running multi-stage lines — crushing, washing, mixing, pressing, sequenced assembly — with belts, funnels, chutes and brass tunnels, and you are measuring throughput in items per minute. There are no item pipes to bail you out; Create's own logistics *are* the puzzle. Late chapters add fluid handling, kinetic power at scale (steam engines didn't exist yet; you'll build windmill and water-wheel farms and eventually furnace engines), and the shuttle itself.

**Time.** 100–200 hours for most players, in short focused sessions — "production chains get designed on paper before they get built" is the recurring description.

## Why it still matters

It taught the genre how to gate. The core-item trick, the chapter structure, the "mass-produce this component" loop, and the ethos that the *pack* — not a mod — is the game, all trace directly to A&B. It is also stable, light and complete, which is more than can be said for many packs three times its age.

## Why it's A, not S

Two reasons. First, it's frozen on 1.16.5 with a Create version that predates trains, steam engines, the display link/redstone overhaul and most of the modern Create QoL; for a Create pack that's a real loss. Second, it is unwelcoming to Create newcomers — the quest book assumes you know what a mechanical press is for — which is why Astral now holds the "learn Create here" crown. Neither is a flaw in the design, both are facts of 2026.

## How it compares

- **vs Create: Astral (Fabric 1.18.2):** Astral is guided and gentler, with rockets and planets; A&B is stricter and purer.
- **vs Create: Arcane Engineering (Forge 1.18.2):** an unofficial "A&B plus magic" — two parallel quest lines (tech via PneumaticCraft/Thermal, magic via Ars Nouveau/Forbidden & Arcanus) that converge. Harder, larger, also frozen (2023).
- **vs All of Create (NeoForge 1.21.1):** modern Create 6 with 50+ add-ons and a quest book; more content, less designed constraint.
- **vs Create: Prepare to Dye (Forge 1.20.1):** the closest modern spiritual successor in *difficulty* — heavy custom recipes around dyes and colour; check it out if you finished A&B and want harder.
- **vs GTNH / Nomifactory:** different scale entirely; A&B is a 150-hour puzzle, not a lifestyle.

## Verdict

If you already enjoy Create and want to be pushed, Above and Beyond remains the best pure automation campaign in modded Minecraft, and its 1.16.5 vintage matters far less than you'd think once you're staring at a belt line trying to hit 30 precision mechanisms a minute. A-tier: the design is S, the era and the on-ramp are not.
