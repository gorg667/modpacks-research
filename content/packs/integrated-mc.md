## Overview

Integrated MC is built by the author of **Integrated Dungeons and Structures**, and the pack is the mod's philosophy scaled up: everything should *belong*. Structures are constructed from the pack's own decorative and functional blocks — Quark, Supplementaries, Create-powered doors, traps and puzzles — and populated with its mobs (Alex's Mobs, Cataclysm bosses in Integrated Cataclysm). Vanilla villages are disabled in favour of Integrated Villages; strongholds and Dungeons Arise are rebuilt as Integrated variants with trial spawners, keys and vaults. Recipes and loot tables reference each other so the pack reads as one game, and even mods you add tend to show up in worldgen.

It's the pack Reddit reaches for when Prominence II frustrates someone: "the mods are pretty well integrated… the structures are grand, massive and built very nicely… lots to do." It offers less *campaign* than the RPG packs and no talent tree; what it offers is a beautifully coherent adventure world.

## Verdict

A-tier: the cohesion benchmark for adventure packs. If mismatched mod soup is your pet peeve, start here.
