## Overview

Pixelmon is the mod that made Pokémon-in-Minecraft a genre, and its official modpack is the second most-downloaded pack in CurseForge history. Its strengths are fidelity and depth: realistic models, 900+ species, competitive-grade mechanics, and years of accumulated features and servers. Its weaknesses are the version lag (1.16.5 remains home) and an art style that, next to Cobblemon's Minecraft-native voxel models, now reads as dated to many. In 2026 it's the nostalgia and fidelity pick; Cobblemon-based packs are where new players go.

## Verdict

B-tier: still a great Pokémon experience for those who prefer its look, but no longer the default.
