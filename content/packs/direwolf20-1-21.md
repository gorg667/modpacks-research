## Overview

The Direwolf20 pack has existed in one form or another since 2012, and the 1.21 edition keeps the formula: a broad, stable, *personally curated* kitchen sink — Mekanism, AE2, Create, Ars Nouveau, Occultism, Botania, Powah, Modular Routers and the rest — with no quest book, updated quickly as mods port, and accompanied by Direwolf20's own weekly series. Reddit's summary is fair: "the latest mods but no questline at all — for people who just want to be up to date and explore." If ATM10's 500 mods and catalogue quests feel like clutter and you already know your way around, this is the cleaner sink.

## Verdict

A-tier for its audience: the veteran's kitchen sink. Beginners should start elsewhere.
