## Overview

Create: Arcane Engineering (CAE) is the community's answer to "what if Above and Beyond had magic and 1.18?" It reuses A&B's structural ideas — chapter gates, core items, mass-produced components — and layers a second tree on top: Ars Nouveau spellcraft, Forbidden & Arcanus artefacts and magical materials whose recipes demand tech inputs, while tech recipes increasingly demand magical ones. PneumaticCraft's pressure systems and the Thermal series broaden the industrial side well beyond A&B's Create-only toolkit, and Create itself is the 1.18.2 version with trains, steam engines and modern logistics.

The 2026 Create-pack comparison guide's summary is accurate: "for those who want magic in the mix… two quest lines that run in parallel and converge at the end… high skill; the crossed magic-tech recipes require juggling several systems at once." The community difficulty threads put it around E6E level — comfortably in expert territory.

## Play notes

- Progression is freer than A&B (two branches to alternate between when one stalls) but less guided than Astral.
- Expect PneumaticCraft pressure chambers feeding Ars enchanting apparatus feeding Create sequenced assembly. If that sentence excites you, this is your pack.
- 5–6 GB RAM; light and stable. Frozen in a *finished* state, which is the good kind of frozen.

## Compared

**A&B** is purer and stricter; **Astral** is gentler and guided; **All of Create** is modern and broad; **CAE** is the deepest and hardest. Play them in roughly that order.

## Verdict

A-tier expert Create pack, and the natural graduation from A&B. It loses a point for being frozen and for a smaller support ecosystem, not for design.
