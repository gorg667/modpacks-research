## Overview

Create 6 landed in early 2025 and changed the mod: factory gauges, packages, stock tickers and frogports turned Create into a proper logistics system, and the add-on ecosystem exploded to match. **All of Create** collects that ecosystem — the trains suite (Steam 'n' Rails), building add-ons (Copycats+, Design n' Decor), utility add-ons (Crafts & Additions, Enchantment Industry, Connected, Dreams & Desires), and dozens more — on NeoForge 1.21.1, and adds a quest book that walks you through what each one does. With 1.4 M downloads by mid-2026 and monthly updates, it is the trending Create pack and the default answer for "I want modern Create with some direction."

## What it isn't

It is not an engineering campaign. There are no chapter gates forcing you to mass-produce mechanisms, no disabled pipes, no space shuttle. The quest book is a *syllabus*; the pack is a Create sandbox with breadth. The 2026 Create-pack guide places it exactly there — the "long-term multiplayer first" pick alongside custom server packs on Create 6.

## Performance

8 GB allocated is the realistic minimum; 10 GB for big bases. Large contraptions and long train networks are the usual Create server load, so budget 10–12 GB server-side for groups.

## Verdict

The best *broad* Create pack, and the right choice if you want everything current with light guidance. Pair it with A&B or Liminal Industries if you later want to be tested. A-tier.
