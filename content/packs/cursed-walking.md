## Overview

The Cursed Walking was the survival-horror story of 2026: a May 11 content drop (two endgame regions, faction reputation, an early-game difficulty rebalance) pushed it to #3 in the world behind ATM10 and StoneBlock 4, and hosting sites started calling it "the defining survival-horror pack of the year." Its formula is simpler and harsher than DeceasedCraft's: zombies spawn in daylight, climb over each other to reach you, **horde every ten days**, can infect you, and the apocalypse advances in **phases** that add runners, brutes and worse. You loot cities, bunkers and labs for Timeless-and-Classics guns and Marbled's armour, and eventually find the map to the drill room that drops you into **the Depths** — a custom endgame dimension with its own biomes, structures and creatures.

It's lighter (4–6 GB), more open-ended, and rougher: its cities are admittedly placeholder-quality with a rework planned for v5, bosses arrive in v4, and a series of balancing updates is on the roadmap. Approved variants cover both ends — **Lite Edition** for weak PCs, **Nightmare** for a much harder remix.

## Verdict

A-tier for tension and momentum: if DeceasedCraft is the designed apocalypse, Cursed Walking is the frantic one, and its update cadence in 2026 has been excellent. Expect rough spots and a city rework still to come.
