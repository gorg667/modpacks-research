## Overview

ATM9 is what most people mean by "a modded server" in 2023–25, and in 2026 it's still the fifth most-downloaded pack ever. Its strength is timing: 1.20.1 became the most complete Forge modding version in history, so ATM9 has *more* mature mods than ATM10 (Blood Magic, Thermal series, EnderIO, and a long tail of 1.20-only projects). It's ~20% lighter than ATM10 and its skyblock variant, **ATM9: To the Sky**, is a popular alternative start. Everything said in the ATM10 review about design applies here. If your PC or your mod wish-list points to 1.20.1, this is the pick; otherwise ATM10 is the future.

## Verdict

A-tier: the best 1.20.1 kitchen sink and a legitimate alternative to its successor.
