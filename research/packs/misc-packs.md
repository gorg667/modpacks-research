# Misc packs — research notes

## Homestead (by tokimi)
Source: https://modrinth.com/modpack/homestead (also CF "homestead-cozy")
- Fabric (uses create-fabric, Beautify: Refabricated). MC version 1.20.1 or 1.21.1 (modready says both — Modrinth lists mods available on both; treat as Fabric 1.20.1/1.21.1). 
- Custom worldgen (Tectonic, Geophilic, Nature's Spirit, Oh The Biomes We've Gone), YUNG's structures, Repurposed Structures. Extensive questbook w/ themed chapters (homesteading, wild exploration, deep mining, magical secrets); no forced path.
- Bundled shaders: Complementary Reimagined (3 presets), Nostalgia, MakeUp UltraFast, Potato, Super Duper Vanilla.
- Mythic Upgrades gem tools (Ruby, Topaz, Peridot, Sapphire, Jade, Aquamarine, Ametrine); Farmer's Delight + Let's Do mods; custom Homestead mod w/ tameable Mushlings & Fernlings; mounts; mini-bosses; Golden Leaf artifact; Chipped, Twigs, Cluttered, Beautify decor; Sophisticated Backpacks, Iron Chests Restocked; Create (tailored), Tom's Simple Storage, Wireless Redstone. 4+ months dev. 4-6GB.

## FTB OceanBlock 2
Source: r/ftb review (48 upvotes, Mar 2025), FTB page (Feb 14 2025)
- NeoForge 1.21.1. Story: escape pod crashed on water planet. ~36h to complete all side quests. Light on grind/perf. 4 main chapters; custom dungeons (underwater glass domes, seabed bosses), airdrops (OP rewards), gambling/loot boxes, The Rift (dark dimension below 150m needing MK2 suit; Mage robe invisibility trick), NauTec custom mod (finicky), Oritech, Mekanism (nerfed gas burner), AE2 (quantum computer core as final craft), EnderIO, Just Dire Things, PneumaticCraft armor for crush depth, Mystical Agriculture (OP), Integrated Dynamics storage early, Occultism/Ars optional. No Create (released 2 weeks after). Saereth composed menu music "Mystic Depths".
- Critique: lacks gating, airdrops OP, many mods with 1-2 recipes of impact, "crafting simulator". Praise: watery vibe, smooth perf, custom progression, upgradable early tools stay relevant, forces unfamiliar mods.

## Difficulty ladder (r/ftb Sep 2023, top comment)
Easiest→hardest: FTB Academy, FTB University, MC Eternal, Cuboid Outpost, PO3 Normal, StoneBlock 3, Rustic Waters 2, Regrowth/Blightfall (hard combat), E2E, Create A&B, Ragnamod 6, SevTech (grindy end), GreedyCraft Expert, Nomifactory, FTB Infinity Evolved Expert, Multiblock Madness, PO3 Kappa, E6E, PO3 Mythic, Meatballcraft, FTB Interactions, DJ2, DDSS (bad: hard for hard's sake), GTNH.
- Commenter: "e2e > nomi > gtnh" ideal ramp. Rustic Waters II: best non-expert with depth/lore (dev Boolyman).
- SevTech vs DJ2 (2021): SevTech = unique staged caveman→space, polished; DJ2 = balls-to-wall automate everything, grindy, "make your own fun" (singularities 100M RF, awakened draconium 1.8B RF).
