# Create-family packs — research notes
Source: https://topmoddedservers.com/en/guides/quel-modpack-create-choisir (Jul 2026), r/CreateMod, modready
## Create: Above and Beyond
- Official Create team pack. Forge 1.16.5, Create 0.3.2 era (pre-trains). ~260 mods (modready says 260; wiki says less — CF "large amount of exclusive blocks/items/fluids + custom scripts").
- ~50 inventions/chapters to mass-produce; culminates in space shuttle assembly. 100-200 hours. No longer updated. Benchmark of genre.
- Demanding; best for players who know Create basics. 6GB RAM.
## Create: Astral
- Fabric 1.18.2, v2.1 released 2025, 1.4M+ downloads. Outstanding quest book; progression from sawmill to rockets; custom Moon/Mars/Mercury (Ad Astra), overhauled worldgen. 80-150h. Best Create entry point. 4-6GB.
## Create: Arcane Engineering
- Forge 1.18.2, 197 mods. A&B-inspired + Ars Nouveau, Forbidden & Arcanus, PneumaticCraft, Thermal. Two parallel quest lines (magic, tech) converge. Last update late 2023; frozen but complete. High skill.
## All of Create
- NeoForge 1.21.1, Create 6.0 + 50+ addons, polished quest book, 1.4M+ downloads, v1.8 May 1 2026. 8GB.
## Others to check: Create: Prepare to Dye (1.20.1, Create+dye theme, hard automation), Create Chronicles, Create: Perfect World, Create Live 5, Liminal Industries (backrooms + Create + survival mechanics).
