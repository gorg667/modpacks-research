# Prominence II: Hasturian Era — research notes
Sources: bisecthosting 4.0 Kingdom of Vaaz (Aug 7 2026); CF page; r/ftb "abysmal experience" (346 upvotes, May 2025, 103 comments); modready
- Fabric 1.20.1. CF + Modrinth (Modrinth 1.9M dl). Developer: originally under Luna Pixel Studios publishing; dev split to form Cinderstone Studios (~2025). ~300+ mods. 6-8GB.
- 4.0 "Kingdom of Vaaz" (Aug 7 2026): handcrafted capital city that evolves with quests; voiced NPCs; tutorial; shops/taverns; 2 new classes w/ spellbooks & armor; Primordial endgame armor; reworked Mythic Challenges (instanced arenas, timed bosses, affixes). Earlier: May 11 2026 large patch (endgame storyline, 2 biomes, economy rework); "Void's Invasion" expansion.
- Progression: levels, talent points every 2 levels; 10 fates (classes), 5+ active abilities, 16+ passive talents, 100+ stat talents; artifact/relic weapons w/ own progression; transmog; multiplayer mob scaling; bosses have levels (below level = -10% dmg per level).
- Mods (RPG core): Epic Fight? (No — uses Better Combat), Simply Swords, Spell Engine/Paladins & Priests/Wizards, MineCells, Bosses of Mass Destruction, Mowzie's Mobs + L_Ender's Cataclysm (added ~2025), Botania, Create, AE2, Powah, Tech Reborn, Ad Astra; Modern Industrialization removed/neutered later.
- Criticism (strong, widely upvoted): tech mods clash with fantasy theme; multiple duplicate ores; poor recipe/config integration ("kitchen sink with a story"); crafting-table lag caused by compat mods (Mowzie's x Cataclysm compat); bosses front-loaded/one-shots, Night Lich bad for melee, Gauntlet bugs out of arena; classes barely matter; quests lore-heavy but not instructive; Essential mod blocked; Discord defensive.
- Praise: visuals, atmosphere, "Minecraft Dungeons 2" feel, always know next objective, stats matter, good multiplayer with friends, learn Botania; later bosses better; big 4.0 improvements.
- Alternatives named by critics: Integrated MC (well-integrated, grand structures), Cisco's Fantasy Medieval RPG (no tech, unbalanced one-shots), Craft to Exile 2 (PoE-like), Raspberry Flavored (vanilla+), Homestead, Legendary Edition, Summoner's Delight.
