# RPG / Adventure packs — research notes

## Craft to Exile 2
Sources: pcgamesn Aug 9 2026 (2.0 Atlas update); CF; logicservers review Nov 2025; r/ftb
- Forge 1.20.1 (check), by mahjerion (GitHub Craft-to-Exile-2). Core: Mine and Slash ARPG mod. PoE/Diablo 2 inspired.
- Classes, talent trees, ascendancies (Blood Mage, Arcane Elementalist, Bard summoner, Crit Berserker; new: Sanguimancer, Cryolancer), randomized loot, dodge roll, uniques/mythics, runewords, support gems, gear crafting.
- Campaign: 5 acts, 250+ quests, dungeons, shops, epilogue. Endgame at Act 5 / level 50: mapping system (instanced repeatable dungeons per biome), 2.0 adds Atlas web + Atlas passive tree, pinnacle "apex" boss, Shrines, Strongboxes, imprisoned monsters, 5 new map types, 25 crafting mats.
- Reddit: "kinda like PoE", some find progression unengaging/"buns"; others "good, strong progression, satisfying builds". Hub-based; auto-draw for bows patch.

## Vault Hunters 3rd Edition
- Forge 1.18.2. By Iskall85 team. Update 21 available (2026). Action RPG roguelike; procedurally generated Vaults; 25 artifacts to reach final challenge; talents/abilities/expertise; Vault gear; research system to unlock mods (Botania, Create, Mekanism, etc. gated by knowledge points); Sky Vaults skyblock variant; Vault Island world type; 18 vault themes.
- Wold's Vaults (CF, updated Aug 15 2026): unofficial expansion built on VH3 U16+, adds optimization mods, addons (Vault Filters, QoL Hunters), new objectives, weapon types, uniques, catalysts, augment table, ParCool + Better Combat, extra questbook, planned endgame; criticized for messy UI. World save convertible from VH3.
- Strong multiplayer/streamer culture (Vault Hunters SMP). Grind-heavy loop; best "shared goal" pack for groups.

## Meatballcraft: Dimensional Ascension
Source: r/ftb announcement (749 upvotes, Mar 2024, 171 comments), CF/miraheze wiki
- Forge 1.12.2 (Cleanroom loader → Java 21/22 supported). 374 mods. Expert tech-RPG; 4+ years dev; still "beta" (endgame extending, GTNH-style). 9 chapters (10th in dev), each with lore + questline; ~70 dimensions; ~60% tech/40% RPG w/ early magic (Thaumcraft, Astral Sorcery, Botania, Blood Magic). Massive custom multiblocks; puzzles w/ hidden recipes hinted in worldgen; custom bosses/NPCs (Meatball Man). World scales harder per chapter. Void world for base early ch2.
- Design praised: inefficient early methods → efficient later; thresholds gate mods but internal recipes untouched; staged gating (dip toe, come back). Balanced around infinite resource gen early (ProjectE, Mystical Agriculture, Chickens) — challenge is complex automation, not grind ("200 million diamonds").
- Completion: ~25 finishers as of 2024, all but 3 took >1000h; fastest <500h. 95% don't pass chapter 4. Needs 10GB+ RAM + specific JVM args; no shaders (custom perf setup incompatible w/ Optifine). One team base per server recommended.
- Criticisms: 1.12.2, structure clutter (improved via pillar fork), early mobs harsh (~20h), goofy tone (horror clowns), heavy exploration vs automation for some tastes.

## DawnCraft
- Forge 1.18.2, 200+ mods, souls-like RPG (Epic Fight), custom questline, reputation system, dozens of animated bosses, official wiki. Top-10 CF downloads. Known for jank/difficulty & performance issues; lock-on combat; villager quests to unlock abilities (e.g., can't use shields/anvils until quests).
## RLCraft
- Forge 1.12.2, v2.9.3 (2023) last; 169 mods; 29M downloads (most ever). Thirst, temperature, Lycanites mobs, Ice & Fire dragons, skill leveling gating gear (Level Up Reloaded), First Aid body parts, Locks, Battletowers; brutal early game; "rite of passage"; Java 8; 4-6GB. Shivaxi creator. Community: 2.9.3 Trials update; RLCraft Dregora variant (custom worldgen).
## Roguelike Adventures and Dungeons 2
- Forge 1.18.2 (RAD2), dungeon crawling, RPG elements, Epic Fight optional; "exhausting" long-term for some.
## Integrated MC
- 1.20.1 Forge, Integrated series (Integrated Dungeons and Structures — IDAS), grand structures, well-integrated mods; praised as anti-Prominence.
## Cisco's Fantasy Medieval RPG [Ultimate]
- 1.20.1, Skyrim-like, no tech, custom weapons/origins/skill tree, 200+ quests; unbalanced one-shots.
