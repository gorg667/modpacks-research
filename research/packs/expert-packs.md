# Expert / GregTech-family packs — research notes

## GTNH (GregTech: New Horizons)
Sources: wiki Beginner Tips https://wiki.gtnewhorizons.com/wiki/Beginner_Tips ; version history; r/ftb threads
- MC 1.7.10, Forge. 10+ years of dev (started 2015). Latest stable 2.8.4 (2025-12-23); 2.9.0 betas ongoing. 2.7.0 (Dec 2024) was largest update ever (Processing Arrays removed, new multiblocks).
- Philosophy: every recipe greg-ified incl vanilla (a door needs advanced tools). Heavy reading required; quest book word-dense.
- Tiers: Stone, Steam, LV, MV, HV, EV, IV, LuV, ZPM, UV, UHV, UEV, UIV, UMV, UXV, MAX. Each tier 4x power of previous.
- Completion estimate (wiki): ~1,600 hours team, ~2,500 hours experienced solo. Stargate: 4,000–8,000+ hours (one player: 3,990h total to clear Aug 2025). "Journey not destination."
- Startup: 3–5 min high-end, 30 min low-end. DO NOT use CurseForge launcher (broken/out of date). Use Prism/MultiMC/ATLauncher/Technic. Java 17-21+ supported via lwjgl3ify — recommended for perf.
- Custom worldgen "Realistic Alpha" (BoP + RWG). Ore veins must be hunted; SP harder than server.
- Food: Spice of Life + nerfed food, HP from variety; Healing Axe quest reward. Blood moons, infernal mobs, exploding animals, quicksand, Thaumcraft taint/hungry nodes hazards.
- Mods: Thaumcraft 4, Tinkers, Forestry bees, Railcraft, AE2, IC2, Pam's, Witchery, Botania, Blood Magic, Galacticraft (rockets), EnderIO, OpenBlocks glider, etc.; many custom GTNH-forked mods. Realistically rebuilt/maintained entire 1.7.10 stack.
- Best played on servers/teams. Very active Discord. Extremely polished wiki.

## Difficulty ranking (r/ftb Sep 2024 consensus)
GT Community Pack Modern (intro to GT, not expert) < StaTech Industry (Modern Industrialization, Fabric) < Enigmatica 2: Expert < Monifactory < Nomifactory CEu < TerraFirmaGreg < Meatballcraft < GTNH.
- DJ2: ~500 hours ("six months"), challenging not brutal, "amazing".

## Why no modern large expert packs (r/ftb Jun 2024, 169 upvotes)
- GTNH has 9.5+ years of dev; expert packs = game-director work; MC versions churn; mods don't backport. Modern-ish expert packs named: Decursio Project (1.16.5, ages like SevTech), Chroma Technology / Chroma Endless 2, E6E / E9E, Stacia (2) Expert, FTB Skies Expert, Multiblock Madness 2, Feed the Factory, Frozenopolis, Create: Arcane Engineering, Gravitas 2 (gregified), Plastek (1.19.2, 7 ages, 2900 quests), Supersymmetry (1.12, "next GTNH"), Age of Fate 1.19.2.
- Meatballcraft: 1.12.2 but runs Java 22 via Cleanroom loader; "insane depth", exploration heavy (criticism: too much exploring vs automation), absurd automation ch6+.
- Kitchen sink (ATM) ≠ expert; "untweaked Mekanism" busted.

## Monifactory
- GitHub Omicron-Industries/Monifactory. "Modern reimagining" of Nomifactory on 1.20.1 NeoForge/Forge w/ GTCEu Modern. Normal/Hard/Expert modes (Hard: Monicoins can't be spent, harder Pt/Naquadah lines, focus on GT power gen; Expert = modifier on Hard).
## Nomifactory / Nomi-CEu
- 1.12.2; Nomifactory (GTCE) and Nomi-CEu fork (GregTech CEu). Omnifactory successor. Rocket-free GT progression to creative items; ~DJ2 playtime. Nomi-CEu has Normal/Expert/Hard modes.
## StaTech Industry
- Fabric 1.20.1?, Modern Industrialization core (not GT). Easiest of group; polished questbook.
## TerraFirmaGreg
- TFC + GregTech (1.20.1 Forge). "90% TFC+ for a long while". Rough, very challenging.
