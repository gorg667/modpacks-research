# FTB StoneBlock 4 — research notes
Sources: https://www.feed-the-beast.com/modpacks/130-ftb-stoneblock-4 ; r/ftb finished-review thread (150 upvotes, Nov 2025) https://www.reddit.com/r/feedthebeast/comments/1p7jm8n/ ; modready May 2026
- Released Oct 29 2025 (FTB page); ModReady lists "May 6 2026" (likely CurseForge/major update) — treat release as late Oct 2025.
- MC 1.21.1 NeoForge, Java 21. ~200+ mods. 6-8GB RAM.
- Core custom systems (KubeJS): World Engine (15+ multiblock upgrades, "town hall" leveling), Vaults (handcrafted trial dungeons: puzzles, combat, endurance; incl. a Portal-gun vault, Pigstep dance party), Echoes (holographic NPC guides: Guidance, Machinist, Quartermaster — shops/trades), Garden of Stone hub w/ 5 starting islands, 6 Foundation Chapters w/ branching paths (complete 2 of 3 to advance), Unearthers (villager-in-glass-cube sieve alternative; feed/upgrade villager; new professions Archaeologist/Geologist/Dimensionalist), brush sifting early.
- Mods: FTB Quests, Team Bases, Replication (buffed), Create, Mekanism, Ars Nouveau, Industrial Foregoing, Occultism, Draconic Evolution, Twilight Forest, Chickens, Fluid Cows, Oritech, Powah, AE2 (channels x2), Apotheosis (gated behind Gateways of Eternity), Just Dire Things, Actually Additions, Sophisticated Storage (upgrades gated behind shop — controversial), Tempad, Malum, Immersive Engineering, Xycraft, ProjectE(?) red matter armor mentioned.
- Endgame: Chapter 6 infinity items/creative challenges (added in 1.1+/1.2). Completion ~100 hours.
- Praise: World Engine progression feel, vaults, unearthers refreshing, "a modpack not Create-and-friends", smooth up to endgame, polished camera moves; even less skilled players can enjoy.
- Criticism: endgame progression wall (time liquid 10 buckets/item), magic mods (Occultism, Ars, Malum) barely required, over-gating (Sophisticated Storage upgrades in shop), random loot rewards can trivialize (orange Apotheosis gear, portal guns), some think World Engine + teleport removes claustrophobic SB feel, random dungeons weak vs story vaults, chickens make resources trivial, bugs in some versions (1.6 vault bedrock).
- Server: needs enable-command-block=true.
